# 🎨 Artist Public Profile – Interaction & Visibility Upgrade (FINAL TASK)

## 🎯 Goal

Finalize the **Artist Public Profile** so it is fully shareable, open, and valuable for artists — allowing anyone to discover, view artworks, and contact the artist **without login barriers**.

This task **closes the Artist Dashboard phase** for now.

---

## 1️⃣ Remove Contact Restriction (Critical)

❌ Remove the text:

> "Log in as a Designer or Gallery to contact this artist."

This restriction must be completely removed from the **Artist Public Profile**.

---

## 2️⃣ Add Public Contact CTA

➕ Add a **Contact Artist** button next to **View Artworks** on the public profile header.

### Behavior:

* Visible to **everyone** (anonymous visitors, designers, galleries, external visitors)
* No login required
* Opens a **Contact Artist form** (modal or inline)

### Contact Form Fields:

* Name
* Email
* Message

### Submission:

* Sends message to the artist’s email
* (Optional / future-proof) Also stores message in RoomVibe inbox

---

## 3️⃣ Display Artist Links on Public Profile

On the Artist Public Profile, display **all links entered in the artist dashboard**.

### Supported links (V1):

* 🌐 Website
* 📷 Instagram
* 📘 Facebook
* 🎵 TikTok

### Rules:

* Show only links that are filled in
* Display as clean icons or labeled links
* Open links in a new tab

> If the artist entered a link, it is considered intentional and allowed.

---

## 4️⃣ Add Likes to Artworks (Public)

➕ Add a **Like (❤️)** feature under each artwork on the public artist profile.

### Requirements:

* Likes are public
* No login required
* Show total like count per artwork

### Notes:

* Basic protection is sufficient (cookie / IP-based)
* No user profiles or advanced social logic needed

---

## 5️⃣ Explicitly Excluded (Future Phase)

❌ Do NOT implement comments yet

* Comments are planned for a later phase
* This task is **Likes only**

---

## 6️⃣ Product Philosophy (Important Context)

RoomVibe **must not block or gate artist visibility**.

The Artist Public Profile is:

* A shareable mini website
* A portfolio page
* A discovery and contact hub

Artists should be free to:

* Share their profile link anywhere
* Be contacted by anyone
* Benefit from maximum exposure

---

## ✅ Completion Criteria

This task is complete when:

* Anyone can contact an artist without logging in
* Artist links are visible on the public profile
* Artworks can receive likes publicly
* No role-based or login-based contact restrictions remain

---

🚀 After this task, **Artist Dashboard & Public Profile are considered complete for Phase 1**.
