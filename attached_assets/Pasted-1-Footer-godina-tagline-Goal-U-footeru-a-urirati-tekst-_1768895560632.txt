1) Footer: godina + tagline

Goal: U footeru ažurirati tekst:

© 2025 RoomVibe → © 2026 RoomVibe (ili još bolje: dinamički trenutna godina)

See Art in Your Space. → Visualize Art in Your Space.

Context (LIVE):
Na dnu stranice (footer) trenutno piše “© 2025 RoomVibe” i “See Art in Your Space.”
Treba biti usklađeno s 2026 i copyjem.

Scope (napravi samo ovo):

Pronađi footer komponentu (npr. Footer.tsx ili ekvivalent).

Promijeni tagline u: “Visualize Art in Your Space.”

Copyright:

Preferirano: koristi new Date().getFullYear() da se automatski mijenja svake godine.

Ako je hardcoded: postavi na 2026.

Provjeri da se footer prikazuje isto na:

Homepage

Dashboard pages

Public pages (artist profile, exhibition embed)

Ne raditi:

Ne mijenjati stil/boje/layout footera

Ne raditi refactor drugih komponenti

Acceptance / Stop condition:

Na live footeru piše “Visualize Art in Your Space.” i godina je 2026 (ili dinamički current year).

2) “View Exhibition” gumbi na artist profilu ne rade (Exhibition not found)

Problem:
Na artist public profilu:

gumb “View Exhibition” (u gornjem dijelu profila) vodi na stranicu gdje piše “Exhibition not found”

i donji CTA “View Artist’s 360° Exhibition” također ne radi

Vidim da se otvara ruta tipa /#/embed/exhibitions/1 i vraća “Exhibition not found”.

Goal: Popraviti linkanje tako da oba gumba vode na ispravnu izložbu tog artista.

Scope (napravi samo ovo):

Pronađi gdje se renderira artist public profil i gumbi:

“View Exhibition”

“View Artist’s 360° Exhibition” (donji CTA)

Uspostavi ispravan exhibition target:

Nemoj hardcodati /embed/exhibitions/1.

Umjesto toga, dohvatiti exhibition za tog artista iz backenda (npr. “latest published exhibition for artistId” ili “active exhibition”).

Update routing:

Gumbi moraju otvoriti točan route koji viewer očekuje (embed/public viewer).

Dodaj minimalni fallback UX:

Ako artist nema izložbu, sakrij oba gumba ili prikaži disabled stanje s tekstom tipa: “No exhibition yet”.

Test:

Otvori public artist profil (Irena R. Malbasa) i klikni oba gumba → oba moraju otvoriti realnu izložbu (ne “not found”).

Ne raditi:

Ne dirati 360 engine, UI gumba, dizajn profila, niti dodavati nove feature-e

Ne refaktorirati cijeli routing sustav

Acceptance / Stop condition:

Klik na View Exhibition radi i otvara ispravnu izložbu za tog artista

Klik na View Artist’s 360° Exhibition radi i otvara istu (ili odgovarajuću 360) izložbu

Nema više “Exhibition not found”

Ako nema izložbe: gumbi se ne prikazuju ili su disabled (bez error page)

Referenca (što se vidi na liveu)

Footer: “© 2025” + “See Art in Your Space.”

Artist profil: gumbi “View Exhibition” / “View Artist’s 360° Exhibition” vode na “Exhibition not found”.
